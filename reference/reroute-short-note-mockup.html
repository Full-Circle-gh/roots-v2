Why Option 2

  • Reads as one card, not a warning banner. A divider + small amber dot flags it without the boxed/tinted treatment fighting the card's own border — the "Fulfilled by" tile stays calm.
  • Scales to multiple short items. The stacked item rows just repeat; Option 1's single keyline row gets cramped once a second SKU is added.
  • Thumbnail + SKU + name together — ops can match it to a shelf tag or a picking screen without opening the product page.
