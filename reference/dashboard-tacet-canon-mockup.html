Order of the page (§7): the dashboard answers one question — is everything okay. The Recap answers for the night, the amber row answers for right now, and everything after it is reference. Money leads the rest because it is the first thing a merchant checks when nothing is wrong.

The Dusk budget (§1): one Dusk element — The Recap, whose breathing dot means the automations are alive and whose row dots mean "Tacet did this." The logo's dot in the topbar is part of the mark. Nothing else on the page may be purple.

The grid: six columns, one column per number a block carries. Three numbers take half, two a third, one a sixth. Rows close: Recap 6 · low 4 + stock 2 · today 6 · customers 3 + money 2 + refunds 1 · channels 3 + delivery 2 + invoices 1.

Motion (§4): everything settles once, in reading order, and then the page is still. The day line draws itself like the waveform going flat; its tip is a still ink dot, because the breathing dot belongs to Dusk alone. Reduced motion turns all of it off.

No repeats: every fact has one home. New customers lives in Customers, today's revenue in the hero, the Recap holds what Tacet did overnight, and Channels shows the mix — never the totals again.
