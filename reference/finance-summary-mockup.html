Finance — Summary

Four states of one screen, stacked so they can be compared. Mockup only. Static HTML, hardcoded numbers, no routes, no schema, no data layer, nothing wired. The date range and location controls are chrome and do nothing. English view only — no Chinese strings are composed here; the pairs owed are listed in the report.

The ¥ is a placeholder so the page has a number on it. Currency is per business (tacet-consolidation-decisions.md §0) and there is no currency logic in this file.

The one-location tenant is not being denied a feature; there is nothing to choose between.

Its expense breakdown has no unallocated line either, by construction: with one location every cost belongs to it. The Not location-specific row and the not-included line are the same fact seen twice, and both only exist where the dimension does. Its figures differ from state 1's breakdown because this is a different tenant that happens to share a top line.

How subtraction reads, without leaning on the minus

Three cues, none of them loud. One — the sign has its own 14px column, so every minus lands on the same x and the deductions form a visible vertical column down the left of the figures; the eye picks up an aligned column pre-attentively in a way it does not pick up a glyph. Two — a deducted figure is Graphite, an added or concluded one is Ink. A deduction is not an error and gets no Brick, no Amber, no tint (§1.10, and the same reasoning as "a down delta renders in Graphite, never Brick"). Three — deductions are body weight 400 while the two conclusions are 500 at 18px, so subtraction sits in the layer the eye reads second.

How the staircase reads

Two conclusions, two hairlines, one landing. Gross profit and the final row are the only 18px Inter 500 things on the card and each is preceded by a 1px rule; every input above them is 15px Inter 400. After Gross profit there is a 16px tread before Operating expenses starts the second flight — that gap is what stops the card reading as one list of five rows. The final row sits on the card's own cream bottom edge, so the terminus is a place rather than just a bigger number, and the type scale never leaves §1.3's six steps.
