Recommendation — Option B (bare glyph). It removes the two loudest offenders (the black shadow and the disc) so the mark simply floats on the paper the way Tacet wants; the cream halo on hover gives enough of a target without adding permanent chrome. Option A is the low-risk pick if you'd rather keep an always-visible affordance.
