Counter till

A cashier rings up a walk-in, takes the money, and the customer leaves with the goods. One screen, two columns, no delivery and no dates.

Android tablet, 9.7″, 2048×1280 landscape, touch only. Drawn at 1024×640 CSS pixels — the tightest plausible reading of that panel at 2× density. Every working target is 52–76px. Colour is the live --td-* block copied verbatim; type is Inter throughout. This is not the desktop admin: the tablet has no rail and no topbar, so none is drawn.

NotesDecisions a reviewer will ask about

  1. Charge is Ink, not Dusk. §1.8 rule 5 was corrected in July on the axis who is acting: Dusk means “tacet did this for you”, and taking money at a counter is the cashier acting. The till therefore spends no Dusk budget at all, the way a Console doesn’t. Dusk appears twice on this whole surface — focus rings (rule 3) and the waiting-for-payment dot (rule 2) — both exempt.
  2. Search is above the grid. Tapping it raises the on-screen keyboard, which covers roughly the bottom half of a 640px-tall screen. A search field placed below the grid would be hidden by the very keyboard it summons.
  3. Quantity controls open inside the line. A modal would cover the order the cashier is reading aloud from, and charge a dismiss tap for every quantity fix. §1.13 permits a modal; the till doesn’t need one.
  4. An out-of-stock tile keeps its slot. Muscle memory is the point of a tile grid, so the tile stays where the thumb expects it, struck through and not tappable, rather than vanishing and reshuffling every tile after it.
  5. Payment is a takeover, not an overlay. The tablet gets turned around. A dimmed sheet leaves the till readable over the customer’s shoulder; a takeover does not.
  6. Fulfilment is Counter, always. No address block, no date picker, no fulfilment picker — the screen cannot express any other kind of order, so it never has to validate one.
  7. The currency mark is not settled. ¥ is Roots’ currency, hardcoded today; the IA blueprint v2.5 and consolidation §0 both call that a defect to remove, not a decision. Treat the symbol as per-business configuration wherever this gets built.
  8. Archetype. None of the seven fits — this is not records arriving, things I made, authoring with preview, configuring, connection status, reading the situation, or analysing a range. Its job is take the money. Under §6 that is an eighth-archetype conversation, and adopting one is a deliberate act, not something a mockup gets to do quietly.
