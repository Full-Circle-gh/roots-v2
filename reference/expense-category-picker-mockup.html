§87 follow-up · the category control

Thirty-one categories on a phone

The Expense screen offers the shop's whole chart of accounts as chips — thirty-one of them, alphabetical, so the amount field is pushed off the top and a driver scrolls past Employee Social Insurance to find Logistics. The layout is the symptom. The cause is that the phone is being handed the bookkeeper's list.

Two different lists wearing one name

Finance needs all thirty-one: rent, wages, social insurance and software fees are real costs and they belong in the books. **An employee will never raise one out of their own pocket.** Nobody pays the landlord from their wallet and claims it back.

A person could claim

  • Logistics & delivery
  • In-house delivery
  • Office supplies
  • Post & courier
  • Maintenance & repairs
  • Tools
  • 其他 Other

Nobody ever will

  • Rent + management fee
  • Salaries & wages
  • Employee social insurance
  • Electricity · Water
  • Software service fee
  • Ingredients · Vegetables
  • Protein powder · Dates

And the screen found something else

Putting the list on a phone made it readable in one go for the first time, and two pairs in it are the same category twice:

Marketing&Promotion 市场推广费用 and 营销 Marketing
Kitchen low cost & non-durable equipment 厨房低值易耗品 and low cost & non-durable equipment 低值易耗品

Every expense filed under one of those splits your monthly breakdown into two lines that read alike — the exact defect the category normaliser was written for, arriving through the vocabulary instead of through a typo. Finance → Categories can already fix it: hide the duplicate, and rows keep their history.

What I would do

A, then B if it is still crowded. A is the honest fix — the phone was being given the wrong list, and one tick per category says which list is which. B is the right control for a long list and can be added later without undoing A. C is the one I would not build: it fixes the average case by making the first use of every new employee the worst case.

1 · Which one?

A is about ten minutes of work plus seven ticks from you. A + B together is an afternoon.

2 · Should I sort by use rather than alphabet?

Independent of the choice above, and free: the shop's most-claimed categories first, so the alphabet stops deciding what a driver sees.

3 · The two duplicate pairs

Say the word and I will hide 营销 Marketing and the unprefixed 低值易耗品 — existing rows keep their category and their history.
