Why two views, not two pages

  • Same orders, one toggle. The card view is for looking after an order. The batch view is for filing tax. They're the same list wearing different clothes — never a separate section to keep in sync.
  • Everything the tax system wants is on the line. Title, tax number, email, amount. Tick, copy, paste. No drawer to open forty times.
  • The two that still need you stand out. Amber "To issue" is the only thing that isn't quiet green — filter to those on a busy day.
