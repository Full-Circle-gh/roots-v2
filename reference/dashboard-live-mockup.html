Built from scratch, from the bento

Every card declares a few shapes it may take. A packing solver picks one per visible card so each row fills the six-column grid — whatever you hide or reorder.

Content is designed for the shape, not stretched: a small card is the headline number; a large one earns the chart, the list, the breakdown. Flip "Something needs you" to watch Inventory reach for a bigger slot.
