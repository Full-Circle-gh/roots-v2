Nothing here is new. Every label, every Chinese string and every component already ships in public/retail-admin/08-customers.js. No string was composed for this mockup, so nothing is owed to docs/tacet-delivery-vocabulary.md.

Dusk count: the three move markers are mockup annotation, not product. In the built drawer there is zero Dusk, as today — a customer record is the customer acting, not the AI.

The one open question the joined card raises: Notes & tags is read-write and Orders by month is read-only. Sharing one border puts a Save button inside a card that is half chart. The alternative is two cards kept adjacent by rule rather than by border. The mockup shows the joined version because it is the one that cannot be undone by a later insertion.
