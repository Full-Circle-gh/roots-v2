Mobile site bar — hamburger menu options

Below 768px the bar is wordmark left and a three-line button right (replacing the lone Menu pill). Tap opens a window with the same seven destinations as the computer bar: the six section links, then Menu for the Ramada QR menu. Each pair shows closed, then open.

Inside the window: New to China · Our products · Our location · Social · Our story · Canton Fair · Menu (Ramada). A section link scrolls and closes the window. Menu opens the Ramada menu (same phone-frame overlay as today on a computer).

Panel under the bar

The sticky bar stays visible. The list drops down and covers the page. Feels attached to the header; quick to scan. Menu pill pinned at the bottom of the panel.
