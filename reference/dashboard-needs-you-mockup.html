The proposal

Recommended

Can you still move them? Yes, and better than now. Today the two cards are locked inside one "Needs you" block — you drag the pair or neither. After this, the low-stock list rides with the Inventory block, which you already drag and hide like any other. There is no second thing to arrange, because there is no second card.

Are both still necessary? The list, yes — it is the only place product names appear. The card, no. "All clear" was written when the blocks had no footers; now every block ends with its own sage line, and Tacet's rule is that silence means fine. A card that exists to announce that three things are fine is the loudest quiet thing on the page.

What "Needs you" becomes. A row that only exists when Delivery, Refunds or Invoices actually need you — the same cards as today, minus stock, minus All clear. On a calm morning the heading is not there at all.
