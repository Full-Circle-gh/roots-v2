What changed, and why

Shown straight away — the three real jobs

  • Live status — read-only context ("2 active, 1 upcoming"), not a setting, but the thing people check first. No control, just an answer.
  • Layout — the 6-way style choice. Still one dropdown; this one genuinely needs to be a dropdown, not tiles, since there are 6 options with real visual differences.
  • Title and Max products shown — the two things that change per campaign.

Moved into "More options", grouped by heading

  • moved Content — Small heading · Button text · Button link · Empty-state text · Hide-when-empty
  • moved Layout details — Card layout · Energy
  • moved Motion — Urgency · Entrance animation

The 7 colour pickers get a drawer of their own

Seven side-by-side colour dropdowns was the single worst offender — nobody needs all seven to change the sale title. They're now a second-level, closed-by-default disclosure inside More options, titled plainly and marked "all optional."

Cut

  • cut Card corners — this set the same corner_radius value as the universal Frame/radius/width section at the bottom of every block. Same setting, shown twice; removed the duplicate here.
