Product page options
Option A. A short list of sections on the left. Click one, see only that one. The right column never moves. The page remembers the section you had open. Recommended
Sales, shipping, flags and notes stay on the right.
Option B. Everything on one page, but each section is a card that folds to one line. The left list jumps down the page. Folded cards stay folded next time. Less clicking than A, more scrolling.
Fold what you never touch. It stays folded.
Option C. The dock's tab strip, carried onto the page and widened. One section at a time. Closest to what exists today and the cheapest to build. Tabs run out of room once a tenant renames or adds sections.
Settings › Products › Product page. An admin sets this once for the whole business. Drag to reorder. Switch off what this business never uses. Every operator sees the same page. Works the same under A, B or C.
