The read解读

written from this period’s numbers由本期数据生成

A growing month, with one thing to watch. Revenue is up 12% and orders up 8%, but the average order slipped 3% — more people are buying, each spending a little less. Two thirds of the money still arrives through WeChat Pay.

The number I’d look at first: 66 cancelled orders — 22% of everything placed, most of them from Meituan. Even winning half of those back is worth more than any campaign this size.

Nearly every buyer this month was new, and only 9% return in their second month. You’re good at finding customers; keeping them is the next thing to build.

这是增长的一个月，但有一处值得留意。营业额上升 12%，订单上升 8%，但平均客单下降 3% —— 买的人更多了，每单花得略少。三分之二的收入仍来自微信支付。

最该先看的数字：66 单取消 —— 占全部下单的 22%，大多来自美团。哪怕挽回一半，也胜过同等规模的任何推广。

本月几乎所有买家都是新客，第二个月仍回购的只有 9%。拉新你已经做得不错，留客是下一件要做的事。
