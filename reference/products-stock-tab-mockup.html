The order, before the layout
The tab is called Stock, and today you scroll past a sourcing row, a sizes row, a paragraph, a full-width button and a SKU field before reaching a single number. Every option below uses this order instead — what you came to change first, what you rarely touch last.
Most → least important
  1. On hand — the total, and how it splits by store
  2. The counts — per size, per store; where you type
  3. Low-stock alert — when to warn you
  4. When out of stock — what a shopper sees at zero (was full-page only)
  5. Sourcing — who holds it; changes almost never
  6. Sizes — add or rename; a jump to the full page
Moved out SKU goes to Basics. It is how you identify the product, not a fact about its stock, and it sat directly above the counts pushing them down.
Today
Four blocks of chrome before any stock, and the counts themselves are the least finished thing in the drawer.

Faults
  • Column headers are 10px uppercase in the mono stack — which is Inter, so it is the banned uppercase
  • Variant names are weight 600; only 400 and 500 exist
  • Count boxes stretch the full column for a two-digit number
  • SKU is identity, not stock, and it pushes the counts down
  • Nothing says the total: 179 is nowhere on screen
  • "Stock & status" — the status field is gone
A · Counts first, in a tight grid — recommended
The total leads, the grid sits directly under it, and everything that rarely changes drops to summary rows at the bottom. The grid keeps size × store comparable at a glance, and the boxes are only as wide as the numbers they hold.

Fixed in all three
Column headers are sentence case at 13px weight 400 — they were 10px uppercase in --f-mono, which is remapped to Inter, so it was uppercase Inter, the exact thing the ban exists for. Variant names drop from weight 600 to 500. The count boxes are sized to their content instead of stretching. The whole matrix moves off the legacy --line / --text-1 / --text-4 tokens onto --td-*, so it finally follows the theme. And the card stops being called "Stock & status" now that the status field is gone.
