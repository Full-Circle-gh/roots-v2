The phone page, redrawn.

Same words, same pictures, same address. The type is set in real pixels, and anything that was sharing the width now stacks.

Recommendation

Build this as the phone layout of the website only. The shop and the mini app keep the 750 scale. Same words, same pictures.

Why it feels small today

The website borrows the mini app’s ruler. Type is a fraction of 750, and the opening block stays two columns on a phone. On a 390px screen that comes out as:

  • Opening lineabout 22px, in a column roughly 160px wide
  • Opening paragraphabout 11px
  • Small headingabout 9px. “New to China?” is that one.
  • Fact sentenceabout 12px, in half the band
  • A stepthe number is about 33px, the sentence about 12px

Type on this page

  • Opening line38px Bear Serif, across the full width
  • Section line32px Bear Serif
  • Reading17px Aleo. A step and a fact sentence are 16px.
  • Small heading13px Toni Grotesk, capitals, forest
  • The four facts40px League Gothic, lime on forest, with a short rule on the heading
  • Step numbers and pricesLilita One. Bear’s own digits are a thin face and do not match the letters.

What the brand already says

  • Oat ground, one forest band, one lime band. Ink for the lines.
  • The filled button is forest. The second button is an outline. Buttons stay pills. Cards keep the 20px corner and the 2px line.
  • Characters are the cut-outs: the flexing one at the opening, the onion by the steps, the radishes by the dishes. The forest band is left clear so the four lines can be read.
  • English only. The chat button stays a forest circle with a line icon.

On the page itself

  • The photograph is full width. The words sit under it.
  • The four facts are one column. Each sentence has the width of the phone.
  • The dishes are a sideways row of large cards, so a name like “Strawberry Coconut Parfait” stays at 18px.
  • The blank 40px gaps between blocks are gone. Each section has its own padding.
  • The small heading on the opening, “Guangzhou · since 2017”, is made from words already on the page.
  • The Chinese line for a taxi is switched on in the editor and the field is empty, so it is left off. The pin is not saved, so Apple Maps, Google Maps and Amap search by the address.

Our story

The Roots of It All

Asher, founder · 3 min

My dad used to say that a tree with strong roots can weather any storm.

I didn't understand that when I was young. I understood it the moment I started cooking in a tiny kitchen, trying to make food that actually made me feel good.

That kitchen became Roots.

No investors. No business plan. Just a frustration, a stove, and a belief that real food shouldn't be complicated.

This is where it started — and why it still matters.
