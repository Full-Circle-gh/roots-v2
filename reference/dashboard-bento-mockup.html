How it fits

Every card declares a few shapes it may take — a wide strip, a small tile, a tall panel. A packing solver picks one shape per card so each row comes out exactly full, whatever you hide or reorder.

The content is designed for each shape, not stretched: a small revenue tile is just the number; a large one earns the curve and the breakdown.

When something needs you, that card reaches for a bigger slot and the quiet ones settle around it. Flip the switch above to watch.
