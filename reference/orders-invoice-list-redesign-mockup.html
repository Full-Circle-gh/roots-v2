What changed

  • The invoice lives inside its order. Not a floating row underneath — it sits in a tinted zone within the same card, so the tax title and the customer can never drift apart.
  • Ten columns become three facts. Who, where it's going, how it's arriving. Everything else folds into a quiet second line or into the drawer.
  • One status word per order. The green dot tells you it's fine at a glance. "To issue" amber is the only thing that asks for you.
  • Space between orders. A full gap between cards, not a hairline. The eye lands on one order at a time instead of a wall of rows.
