Recording an expense on the phone

A driver has paid ¥38 for a taxi on a work run. Three ways the phone could take that, drawn in Tacet's own type and colour so the choice is made against what the app really looks like. Every option submits the same claim and reaches the same approval list — what differs is the order the questions come in, and how much of the screen the first one gets.

The same in all three

None of this is what you are choosing between. It is what a claim carries whichever screen takes it, so the options differ only where they actually differ.

  • Amount¥, to the fen. Typed, never stepped — nobody taps + thirty-eight times.
  • CategoryThe tenant's own list, from step one. Hidden ones are not offered.
  • Who paidThe company account, or the employee's own money. This is the field that decides whether the business owes somebody ¥38.
  • ReceiptA photo, optional. Reading it automatically comes later.
  • NoteFree text, optional. “Airport run for the Tianhe order.”
  • Date & shopToday and the submitter's own shop, both changeable, neither asked about.

And what happens after

Also identical: the claim is not in the books. It waits in a list the manager works through, and only approval writes the ¥38 into the month's expenses.

What I need from you

One answer starts the build; the other two I can take a default on and you can change your mind when you see it running.

1 · Which screen?

C is my recommendation. It puts the decision that matters for your accounts on the biggest, most learnable control, and it is the only one where the whole claim is two taps and a number.

2 · Is the receipt photo required?

Default: optional. Required means a driver with a dead phone camera cannot record a real expense, and the ones that need proof are the large ones, not the ¥38 ones.

3 · Does “who paid” default?

Default: to my own money, since that is why an employee is recording it at all. A manager on the company account changes it once per claim.
