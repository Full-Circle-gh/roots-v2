Naming the item an awaiting-restock order is parked on

Order TC000518 sits in Awaiting restock and the drawer never says which of its seven products is holding it. Staff open the order, read every line, and still have to guess — or go to Products and check stock one SKU at a time.

The answer is already in the payload: awaiting_restock_pids is written at checkout (fallback_choice = 'wait_for_restock') and reaches the drawer through GET /api/retail-admin/orders/:id, which selects o.*. Four ways to spend it, built on the patterns the drawer already uses for the shipping-store shortage strip.

“How much do I bring in, and is this order about to move?” A line already restocked reads as covered instead of looking still-broken.

Watch for

Build

Needs a backend change

One behaviour worth stating in whichever option ships: the release check in checkAwaitingRestockForRestock() requires every line on the order to be coverable, not just the parked ones. An order can stay held after its named product lands, and staff should not read that as a bug.
