Six faults, and one of them is not styling

Measured against docs/tacet-design-system.md §5 and the app's own sheetActions, which is the pattern a list of actions already has in this file.

1No container, no icon, no affordance. Every other action list in m.html is a bordered card of left-aligned rows with an icon. These are five centred sentences in bare space — they read as prose, and nothing says they can be pressed.
2Labels are clauses, not verbs. §6 says labels are verbs. “Let them earn the ongoing share” is a sentence; “Allow” is a label.
3Brick text floating in a list. §5 gives destructive a Brick outline. Two red rows scattered among three black ones reads arbitrary — and puts Block, which locks a person out, at the same visual weight as Reissue, which is reversible in a tap.
4Two unrelated groups in one stack. Three of these are about referrals and two are about the account. Nothing separates them.
5Orphaned. The block has no heading, so it hangs under “No app errors in 7 days” as though it belonged to the diagnostics line.
6The real error: it is in the wrong place. This is the desktop’s More ▾ menu, lifted whole. But four of the five act on facts already on screen a few rows up — Signed in on 2 devices, Their code, Referring, Ongoing share. A control belongs beside the fact it changes, not in a drawer at the bottom repeating it.
