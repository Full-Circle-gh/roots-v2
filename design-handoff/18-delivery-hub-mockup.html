Delivery — redesigned as a card hub · 配送页改版:卡片中心

Same idea as the Sales-page channel cards: the Delivery page becomes a short grid of cards — one card per topic, each with a live status line and a single button. All the dense forms move to their own sub-page behind that button. JD Logistics and Qingyun riders move here from the Sales page. Four screens below.

Screen 1 — what a brand-new store owner sees. A 2-step strip, grey dots, "Set up" everywhere.
