## §NEW — A STORE-SCOPED OPERATOR MANAGES STOCK STAFF ONLY AT THEIR OWN STORES

**Built 2026-10-01 under §412.5** (§358 left this open). A store's
stock-staff sign-ins are added, removed and reset through
`/api/backend/stores/:storeId/staff` (`routes/backend-team.js`). Before this,
the key `storeStaffCap` let a store-scoped operator do it at any store in the
shop, including stores whose data that operator cannot even see.

1. **The server.** The three writes (add, remove, reset password) check the
   key as before, then `requireOwnStore`:
   - **A store-scoped operator** (`scopeStores` from
     `lib/operator-caps.js`): the `:storeId` must be one of their stores.
     Otherwise they get `403` with *You can manage stock staff only at your
     own stores · 你只能管理自己门店的库存员* (`code: store_out_of_scope`), and
     nothing is written.
   - **A scoped operator with no store at all** manages none.
   - **Unchanged:** an unscoped operator and the owner (even one pinned to a
     store).
   - **The list stays a read,** as before.
2. **The desktop.** Operations → Stores draws *Stock staff* only on the
   operator's own stores, and `_opsStoreStaff` refuses to open another. The
   whole scope is now kept from `/me` (`RA_SCOPE_STORES`, `RA_IS_OWNER`); the
   desktop used to keep only the first scoped store, as the dashboard lock.
3. **The phone.** `stfHere(id)` gives the store list's *Stock staff* button,
   the store screen's row, opening the staff screen, and all three writes. It
   needs the route key, the operator's scope (`state.scopeStores`, kept whole
   from `/me`), and the keys to have loaded. So nothing is drawn or sent while
   `/me` is still loading.
4. **Tests.**
   - `scripts/test_store_staff_gates.js`: the real router. Covers own store
     allowed, another store refused with nothing written (all three writes),
     an unscoped operator and a pinned owner unchanged, the list open, and the
     desktop's gate.
   - `scripts/test_mobile_settings_stores.js`: the phone's doors and writes
     per store, unscoped and owner unchanged, nothing while loading.
