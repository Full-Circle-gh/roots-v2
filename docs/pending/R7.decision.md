## §NEW — THE BUILD LIST'S MONEY IS SENT ONLY ON THE KEYS THAT DRAW IT

**Built 2026-09-30 under §300** (Ash: *"yes , and keep going until you finish all .I approve all"*).
Step R7 of the recipes lane. This is server-side only. It closes a leak found
while building R6: the product build list sent every cost and price to anyone
who could open the product, and so did the recipe-link answer.

1. **Two keys, one for each kind of money.**
   - `products:costs` covers what the product costs: `unit_cost`, and the
     build list's computed total (`cost.unitCost`, `cost.ingredientCost`).
   - `materials:costs` covers what a material costs:
     - each line's `material_cost_per_unit` and `material_buy_price`;
     - `cost_per_unit` and `buy_price` on the recipe bridge's material and on
       each picker row;
     - `costPerUnit` and `lineCost` in the computed cost's lines.

   These are the keys the phone (§300 R6) and the desktop product editor
   already draw on.
2. **The rules are the recipe-cost ones, copied, not re-decided**
   (`lib/recipe-cost-visibility.js`).
   - It fails closed when the caller's capabilities could not be worked out.
   - Keys are deleted, never set to 0.
   - A field is withheld, never the whole request.
   - The computed cost object is filtered by an allow-list. Its counts and
     causes (`hasBuildList`, `ingredientLines`, `zeroCostLines`,
     `unreconciledUnitLines`, `packagingOnly`) stay for everyone.
   - The owner, and a tenant with enforcement off, have both keys and read
     everything, as they already read recipe costs.
3. **Where it applies.**
   - `GET /products/:id/build-lines`.
   - The build list's material picker, `GET /product-build/materials`.
   - The answer to `PUT /products/:id/build-lines`.
   - The answers to `PUT` and `DELETE /products/:id/recipe-link`. These go
     through `stripProductCosts`, the gate every other product read uses.

   The rules live in a new `lib/product-build-cost-visibility.js`. That keeps
   them testable without a database, as their two sibling modules are.
4. **A holder of both keys gets exactly what they got before.** The module
   hands back the same object, and the suite compares the JSON against the
   route mounted without the module.
5. **Saving is unchanged.** The build-list save reads no money from the
   request. It writes ingredient, names, SKU, quantity, unit, notes and the
   kept flag, and the server's own recompute writes the cost. So a
   non-holder's save cannot blank a stored cost; the suite checks this.
6. **What the screens show now.**
   - The phone already drew nothing it may not. For a reader with
     `products:costs` but not `materials:costs`, it now takes the total from
     the server's own figure for the saved list. While unsaved edits are in
     hand it shows a dash rather than adding up rates it no longer has.
   - The desktop's build list shows dashes and "no cost" to a reader without
     the key. That is intended: the figures stop reaching someone who may not
     read them. Its wording for that state is a note for Ash, not part of this
     step.
