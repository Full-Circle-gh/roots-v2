## §NEW — SUPPLIERS COME TO THE PHONE, WITH THE CREDIT SWITCH

**Built 2026-09-30 under §300** (Ash: *"yes , and keep going until you finish all .I approve all"*).
Step P7 of the purchasing lane, the last one. The phone had only *Suppliers we
owe* (§286). It now has the desktop's Suppliers tab and supplier page
(`16-purchasing.js`) apart from CSV import. No server changes were made; every
write is the desktop's request and body.

1. **Suppliers replaces Suppliers-we-owe on Purchasing's tab strip.** Whoever
   could open that tab can open this one (`purchasing:view`). It reads
   `GET /suppliers`, and reads `?all=1` only when *Disabled* is tapped, as the
   desktop does. There are Active / Disabled segments and a search over both
   names, the contact and what they supply. Each row gives what they supply,
   their files, contact, terms, orders, the last order and the rating.
2. **The Suppliers-we-owe card heads the directory, and it is money.** It shows
   only where money shows (`purchasing:view`, failing closed). Its rows open the
   same account sheet as before (`openSupOwed`). An old link to the
   `supowed` tab lands on Suppliers.
3. **New supplier is `POST /suppliers`**, `purchasing:edit`, on its own screen
   with *Save supplier*. The name is the only required field. The server's
   `name_taken` is said in words (*Name already exists*). On success the new
   record opens.
4. **An existing supplier saves itself, as on the desktop.** The same
   `PUT /suppliers/:id?history=supplier.<id>` goes out after a pause, and again
   on leaving the screen. It carries Undo (the visit's start, filed `undo`) and
   Version history (`GET /suppliers/:id/history` in the phone's shared history
   sheet; a restore is filed `restore`). An emptied name is held and not sent.
   The history names what changed by the field's label.
5. **The fields are the desktop's**: who they are, how you pay them (When /
   How as free text with the desktop's four suggestions each), where you order
   (channel, link), rating (after the first save, as the desktop), notes.
   Channel names are the desktop's reach-chip labels, where the desktop's own
   select shows the raw value.
6. **The scorecard**: orders and the last one, products bought before, spend
   all time, awaiting 发票. Spend, awaiting, order totals and the price list are
   money and are hidden where money is (`purchasing:view`, failing closed).
7. **The credit switch comes to the phone** (§300 item 2 overturns the §286
   hold that the switch lives on the desktop). It is `PUT /suppliers/:id/account`
   `{on_credit}`, `purchasing:edit`, always behind a confirm. Turning it off is
   marked as the dangerous way and says the entries are kept. With the switch
   on, the record shows what we owe and opens the account sheet. The
   Suppliers-we-owe empty state no longer sends anyone to the computer.
8. **Disable / Enable** is `PUT /suppliers/:id {is_active}`, then a re-read.
9. **The price list opens Price history** (P6) per product. **Paperwork**: an
   image opens in the viewer, anything else as a file. Upload is the desktop's
   `POST /suppliers/:id/documents` body (kind, label, file name, type, size, the
   file as a data URL), at most 10MB, refused on the phone before sending. Delete
   asks the desktop's question first. **Orders with them** open the order sheet.
   **New order** opens the new-order screen (P2) with this supplier chosen, at
   its first step; nothing is written until it is saved there.
10. **Without `purchasing:edit`** the fields are read-only and no switch, upload,
    delete, disable or undo is drawn; each command refuses as well.
11. **Not built: CSV import** (§300 item 4).

**Found and fixed while building:** the autosave read the draft it was started
with, so Undo and a restore (which replace the draft) would have sent the
unchanged fields. It now reads the live draft; the suite pins both.

**Tests:** new `scripts/test_mobile_purchasing_suppliers.js` (28 cases, fast
shard), running the real autosave engine and history sheet, with every write
asserted on its request. The purchasing orders and invoices suites were updated
for the renamed tab.
