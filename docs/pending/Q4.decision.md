## §NEW — "OPEN EVERYTHING" KEEPS ITS RULE AND NOW SAYS WHICH CHANNELS STAYED CLOSED

**Built 2026-10-01 under §412.4** (Ash: *"as you recommend fix all"*).
"Open everything" (`POST /store-status/master {open: true}`) reopens the online
store, every Meituan shop and every shop's fulfilment. It does not touch a
channel's own cell, so a channel closed at one shop with that shop's own switch
stays closed. That rule is kept. What changes is that the operator is now told
which channels stayed closed, on the desktop and on the phone, instead of
finding them on the grid later.

1. **The server says which.** After an open, the master route returns
   `stayedClosed`:
   - a list of `{storeCode, name, nameEn, nameCn, channels}`;
   - shops come in the grid's order, and channels in its order;
   - it is read after the write with the grid's own reading (`csCellOpen`),
     over the channels each shop serves (`csChannelsAt`);
   - Meituan is left out, because the same write has just queued it open
     everywhere;
   - shops with nothing closed are left out, so an empty list means everything
     opened. Closing returns an empty list.
2. **The desktop's Operations panel** keeps the list from the answer and says,
   under the master button, *Still closed — closed at the shop with its own
   switch: Yuexiu: WeChat, Eleme; Tianhe: Counter sale*. It uses the panel's
   own channel names (`csName`). The line stays until the next write, and is
   repainted on a language switch. The toast is unchanged.
3. **The phone's Open for business card** (§325 X12) does the same, in the
   phone's channel names (`CHAN_META`), and keeps the line until the next
   write. The confirm before the write already said that such channels stay
   closed. It is unchanged.
4. **Nothing about what opens changed.** The suite checks that the closed
   cells are still closed after the open, and that every shop is taking orders
   again.
