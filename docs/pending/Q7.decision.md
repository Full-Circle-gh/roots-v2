## §NEW — ONE CHINESE WORD PER THING: HOLIDAY MODE, ZONES, EDIT SHIP-TO, "YOUR STORE IS OPEN", AND THE DRAFTS CHIP

**Built 2026-10-01 under §412.7** (Ash: *"as you recommend fix all"*).
Five things were named in Chinese two ways, depending on the surface. Each now
has one word everywhere it is shown: the desktop, the phone, the page editors,
the shop, the mini app and the server's own labels. Every occurrence was
grepped first. The mini app and the editors already used the settled words or
do not show these things.

1. **Holiday mode is 假期模式**, the desktop's word and the mini app's. The
   phone's eight 假日模式 change: the summary row and its banner, the confirm,
   the toast, the refusal line and the key's label.
2. **Delivery zones are 配送区**, the desktop's word.
   - The phone's Delivery summary card said 配送范围 in its row and in its
     "zones switched off" warning. Both change.
   - A third word for the same thing, 配送区域, changes where it names the
     operator's zones:
     - the desktop's integration card;
     - the "address not in any zone" error;
     - the permission labels (`lib/capabilities.js`: view, edit and delete
       delivery zones, and the group);
     - the onboarding step;
     - the recycle bin's kind label and its note on both surfaces.
   - **Not changed, on purpose:**
     - 配送区域 where it is the *customer's* delivery area, in the shop's and
       the mini app's address pickers and "outside this order's area";
     - 配送范围 where it means coverage, in the mini app's "not in our delivery
       area" and the desktop's "Coverage & pricing".

     Those are other things, and changing them would make one word name two
     things.
3. **Edit ship-to is 修改收货地址**, the order drawer's word. The desktop's
   three quick panels (parked, expired, rider-failed) said 修改收货信息, and
   change. So does the rider-failure sentence that names the button, on the
   desktop and the phone (`修改收货地址或转为中文地址`).
4. **"Your store is open" is 店铺已开业**, the phone setup guide's word. The
   phone's toast after *Open the doors* and the desktop setup guide's heading
   said 你的店开张了, and change.
5. **The desktop's Drafts chip gains 草稿.** The chip on the Orders list was
   English in Chinese mode, because the page's text translation table had no
   entry for it. It now has one, in both copies of the table
   (`retail-admin.html` and `10-extras.js`). 草稿 is the drafts view's own word
   (`Drafts did not load · 草稿未能加载`).
6. **Kept settled.** The suites that pinned the old words follow the new ones.
   A new fast suite, `scripts/test_one_word_per_thing.js`, fails if a retired
   word comes back on any screen, and checks each settled word where the thing
   is named.
