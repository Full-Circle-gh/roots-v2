## What Tacet is

Tacet is a sophisticated, unified administrative dashboard with silent AI automations for digital merchants. It consolidates fragmented tools — WeChat Mini-Programs, custom websites, mobile apps — into a single point of control. The product is software; what it sells is **operational peace of mind** for business owners exhausted by daily platform jumping.

**The name:** *tacet* is the musical notation that tells a musician: rest — the orchestra plays without you. That is the product promise in one word. The merchant rests. The system plays. Pronounced TAH-set, always written lowercase.

**Tagline:** Runs in silence.
Alternates: "Everything, quietly handled." / "The quiet behind your business." / "One dashboard. Every channel. Quiet."

## Audience

Everyday e-commerce merchants and small business owners — not enterprise ops teams. They are competent but exhausted: they spend their day switching between disconnected tools just to keep their store running. They fear that something is going wrong on a platform they haven't checked today. They don't want more features; they want fewer worries.

**Tacet is designed for this audience of merchants, never around its first tenant's habits.** What the reference shop happens to use or skip is a sample of one; the product ships what its market needs. (The working rule with teeth is `tacet-desktop-patterns.md` §1.1b.)

## Positioning

For e-commerce merchants exhausted by platform jumping, Tacet is the unified command layer that consolidates every channel into one dashboard, with silent AI handling repetitive work. Unlike tools that add to the noise, Tacet removes it.

**The enemy:** Tacet does not compete with any single tool. It competes with the noise itself — seventeen open tabs, notification anxiety, the daily chaos of checking five platforms. All marketing invokes that pain, then resolves it.

**Elevator pitch:** "Merchants today run their business across five disconnected platforms — a WeChat Mini-Program here, a website there, an app somewhere else. They spend their day jumping between tabs just to keep up. Tacet consolidates all of it into one dashboard, and its AI quietly handles the routine work — inventory syncs, order routing, customer replies. In music, *tacet* means the player rests while the orchestra plays. That's the product."

## Mission, vision, values

- **Mission:** Give every merchant one quiet place to run their entire business.
- **Vision:** A world where running an online business feels like listening, not firefighting.
- **Values:**
  1. *Silence is a feature.* Every notification we don't send, every click we remove, is product work.
  2. *Trust is earned invisibly.* The AI acts quietly but always shows its work when asked. Silent, never secretive.
  3. *The merchant is the soloist.* Tacet is the orchestra behind them. We make them look effortless; we never take the spotlight.

## Personality

Three traits, in priority order:

1. **Composed** — never shouts, never uses exclamation marks, never panics.
2. **Quietly powerful** — confidence through understatement, like a luxury watch.
3. **Warm** — human and a little poetic, never cold or robotic.

## Voice — three rules with teeth

1. **State the outcome, imply the effort.** Never describe the machinery.
   - ✗ "Our powerful AI engine leverages multi-platform APIs to synchronize your inventory in real time!"
   - ✓ "Your inventory matches everywhere. Always."
2. **Short sentences. Then shorter.** If a sentence can lose a word, it loses the word. No exclamation marks, ever — even good news stays calm.
3. **Dry warmth.** One small human moment per screen or page, maximum.
   - ✓ "3 issues resolved overnight. You're welcome."

More examples:
- ✗ "Congratulations!! You've successfully connected your store!" → ✓ "Store connected. It's handled from here."
- ✗ "Error: synchronization failure occurred in module 7." → ✓ "Something needs your eye on Orders. Two minutes, we'll walk you through it."
- ✗ "Unlock seamless omnichannel empowerment." → ✓ "One dashboard. Every channel. Quiet."

## Signature copy device — "While you were…"

A recurring format across product and marketing that dramatizes the silent machine without explaining it:

- "While you slept: 214 orders processed, 3 issues resolved. You're welcome."
- "While you were in a meeting: 2 stock-outs prevented."
- "Your store just processed 47 orders. You were having dinner."

## Naming conventions

Product features use plain functional names: Dashboard, Orders, Reports, Channels, Automations. Merchants need clarity first, and a boring name is a kindness.

**The musical layer is reserved for the AI's own voice** — the places where Tacet speaks to the merchant rather than the merchant operating a tool:

- **The Recap** — the daily digest of everything Tacet handled. First thing on the dashboard and app home.
- **The read** — the AI's plain-words analysis of a period. Lives on Reports.
- **Ask** — putting a question to the AI directly.
- **Arrangements** — the automation library (an arrangement is instructions for how the orchestra plays without you).

**The test is not "is it a flagship?" — it is "is this the AI speaking?"** A merchant-facing feature that isn't the AI's voice gets a plain name, however important it is. Orders is the heart of the product and it is called Orders.

This is a narrow door, not an open one. Four names carry the layer today; a fifth requires an AI surface **and** a deliberate decision. Anything more themed is too cute.

## Bilingual note

The brand must work in English and Chinese (WeChat ecosystem). CJK typography: Source Han Serif pairs with Fraunces, Source Han Sans pairs with Inter.

## Story beats for marketing assets

When generating landing pages, decks, or ads, the narrative arc is always: **noise → one line → silence.**

1. Open on the pain: fragmented tools, tabs, chaos (never mock the merchant — the tools are the villain).
2. Resolve visually: many channels converging into one calm line (the logo's waveform settling is this story drawn in a single stroke).
3. Land the promise: peace of mind, evidence via "While you were…" moments.
4. Close quiet: one Dusk CTA, generous whitespace, no urgency tactics.

**The one-line style guide: when in doubt, remove something.**
