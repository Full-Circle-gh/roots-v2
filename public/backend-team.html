Team members · 团队成员

A member's role sets sensible defaults. Open Permissions on anyone to fine-tune exactly what they see and do, and limit them to specific stores. Owner always keeps full access.角色提供合理的默认权限。在任意成员上打开权限,即可精细控制其可见与可做的内容,并限定到指定门店。负责人始终拥有完整权限。

Enforce permissions强制权限
