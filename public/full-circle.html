One platform, six surfaces

Everything a modern retail brand needs to sell.

From the shopper's first tap to the warehouse pick slip — and the money in your account — it's the same connected system.

Storefront builder

A block-based page editor with your brand colours and fonts. Launch flash sales, category tabs and editorial layouts without code.

Native mini-app

A fast WeChat mini-app shoppers already trust — product pages, cart, wallet top-up and a customer 'Me' page, all branded as you.

Order fulfillment

Stores and warehouse staff pick, pack and dispatch from one clean queue. Delivery zones, lead times and slots are all configurable.

POS & stations

Tablet POS for in-store checkout, plus kitchen and restock station shells — installable as PWAs, working hand-in-hand with online orders.

Payments to your bank

Real WeChat Pay (JSAPI v3). Every order and top-up settles to your own merchant account — no platform middleman, no 分账.

Multi-tenant & bilingual

Run one brand or many from a SaaS admin with a guided onboarding checklist. Every surface speaks English and 中文, switched by one toggle.
